# Supplied data inventory and analysis boundary

## Available and readable

- `PupilHG/PHG_20s.mat`, containing the authoritative derived MATLAB object
- 913 contact rows on 146 electrode shafts from 18 participants
- 285 contacts (31.2%) with a non-zero stored signed response
- 177 contacts meeting the historical `RespSig > 0.10` selection rule
- 9,866 channel-pair rows and 12,567 wavelet rows
- MNI coordinates for all 913 contact rows
- legacy manuscript figures, tables, and MATLAB analysis scripts

## Two properties of the derived variables

Both are load-bearing for the analysis design and are documented here because
they were misdescribed in earlier drafts.

- `RespSig` is the proportion of the +/-5 s peri-peak window covered by the
  longest contiguous suprathreshold run (`PupilHG.m:517`, `:533`). It is a
  contiguity criterion, not a permutation p-value.
- `RespAreaNet` is exactly zero for 628 of 913 contacts, precisely those with
  `RespSig == 0`. The variable is semicontinuous, which is why the pipeline uses
  a hurdle model rather than a Gaussian one.

## Not present in the supplied archive

- continuous raw iEEG (`.ns*`, `.nev`, `.nf3`, EDF, NWB, SET/FDT, or MEF3)
- synchronized pupil sample streams and validity masks
- acquisition channel maps and bad-channel annotations
- source anatomical MRI/CT suitable for BIDS
- SOZ, IED, seizure, and sleep/wake annotations
- raw event timing needed for leakage-safe deep-learning validation
- luminance and gaze-position traces

## Consequence

The hurdle models, coverage summaries, within-patient and threshold robustness
checks, descriptive spectral/lag/network views, and electrode localization are
reproducible now. Ripple-state tests, SOZ/IED sensitivity analyses, luminance
and gaze regression, raw-signal pupil prediction, and a raw iEEG BIDS release
are not estimable from this archive. They remain prospective methods, not
reported results.

## External dependency

The MNI template surface is generated by LeGUI's own `LeG_genSurfaces`
(https://github.com/Rolston-Lab/LeGUI), which is cloned outside the repository
and pointed to by `config/local_config.m`. LeGUI's bundled SPM reader depends on
a `spm_slice_vol` mex that is not built for macOS, so the pipeline reads the
tissue probability maps with MATLAB's native NIfTI functions and passes LeGUI
the SPM-style header it expects. The surface algorithm itself is LeGUI's,
unmodified.
